## What's new in 2.1.6

**Works on older phones (Android 8 and 9)**
- Fixed signing in on Android 8 / 9. These phones didn't trust Amizone's security certificate, so the app couldn't connect at all.
- If your phone's web engine is too old for Amizone's human check, the sign-in screen now says so and takes you to the free "Android System WebView" update in the Play Store.
- The login screen scrolls, so the keyboard no longer hides the password box.

**Haptics on every phone**
- Vibration now adapts to your phone's motor, so taps and clicks are felt on phones where they used to do nothing. Settings shows what it detected.

**Smaller touches**
- The quick menu opens and closes faster.
- Long exam names shrink to fit on the Home card instead of being cut off.
