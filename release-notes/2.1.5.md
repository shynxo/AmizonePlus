## What's new in 2.1.5

**Stay signed in**
- Amizone now asks for a human check (CAPTCHA) every time you sign in from scratch. The app keeps your session alive in the background, so you should rarely see it again.
- New **Settings → Stay signed in**: tap *Allow* so your phone doesn't put the app to sleep and end the session.
- If Amizone does ask for the check, a yellow card says so (the app isn't broken). Tap it, tick the box once, and you're back. Your last saved data stays on screen meanwhile.

**About**
- Shows your Amity email and its password from the Amizone dashboard (hidden until you tap). It's stored encrypted, on your phone only.

**Moving around**
- Swipe to change tabs.
- Tap your initials to open Settings & About. It grows out of the avatar, and tapping it again (or the back button at the bottom) closes it.
- Smoother quick menu: rows fade in place, with a soft blurred backdrop. Tap outside to close.
