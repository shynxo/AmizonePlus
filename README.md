# Amizone+

A faster, friendlier Android app for Amity's **Amizone** student portal — attendance,
timetable, exams, results, LMS study material and more, in one place. Sign in once; it
keeps itself up to date.

> Unofficial. Not affiliated with Amity University. Your login is encrypted on your phone
> (Android Keystore) and only ever sent to Amizone itself.

## Features

- **Attendance** per subject, with a real semester plan: planned classes = 15 × (L + T + P)
  from the Programme Structure, how many you can still skip, and how many extra classes
  you'd need when you're short. Pick a target from 75% to 95%.
- **Today**: next class with a countdown, upcoming exams, a week of attendance at a glance.
- **Timetable & exams**, with class and exam reminders and low-attendance alerts.
- **LMS**: every course's slides, notes and assignments, arranged the way teachers uploaded
  them — tap to open PDFs and PPTs.
- **Results / CGPA**, and quick access to the rest of Amizone (feedback, OD, fees, Wi-Fi…).
- A pixel cat and dog who live in the app. Tap, pick up, throw, or carry them between tabs.
- Light/dark theme, haptics, pull-to-refresh with character.

## Install

Download the latest `.apk` from **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** and open it on your
phone (allow "install unknown apps" when asked). After that, the app tells you when an
update is out and installs it in two taps.

## Build it yourself

Android Studio (or `gradlew assembleRelease`), JDK 17. Native Kotlin + Jetpack Compose.
See [GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md) for how the code is laid out and [RELEASING.md](RELEASING.md)
for publishing updates.
